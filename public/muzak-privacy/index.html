Muzak privacy

Muzak is a music player for Apple Music on iPhone. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't sell or share data with anyone.

What stays on your phone

Your Apple Music library, play history, ratings and settings stay on your iPhone. Some of them sync through your own iCloud account (Apple's private iCloud database), which only you can read.

What leaves your phone, and why

  • Apple Music: searching and playing goes through Apple's MusicKit, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Album art: when Apple has no cover for an album, Muzak asks its art finder server for one. The server gets the album's title and artist, plus your IP address as any web request does. It keeps no log tied to you.
  • Share links: when you tap Share, Muzak makes a link to that song or album. Only the song or album goes into the link.
  • Muzak on a Mac (optional): if you pair the phone with Muzak on your own Mac, the two talk to each other on your home network, or away from home through a relay server over HTTPS, protected by a key only your phone and Mac share. The relay passes messages along and doesn't keep them.

Microphone

The microphone is used only if you calibrate speakers with a Mac, to time the sound. Nothing is recorded or sent.
