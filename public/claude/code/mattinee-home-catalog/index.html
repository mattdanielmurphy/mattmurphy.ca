23:15 Plan and audit

You asked for the rest of the new Home on the Mac: everything from the old screen, a grid for each category, a proper search, plus the top padding and the row shift. Worktree: .claude/worktrees/home-catalog, branch claude/home-catalog.

What the old screen has that Home lacks

Old screen is web/app.ts + web/index.html. Home is app/ui/home.js.

Missing from Home, moving now

  • Minimum IMDb filter (minImdb: Any, 6+, 7+, 7.5+, 8+) with its count line and its empty message.
  • On this Mac (d.local). Its ids must be local:<kind>:<title>.
  • Letterboxd and Trakt site buttons, Trakt connect and disconnect (old Browse shelf). Nobody listed these; they would have been lost.
  • Page arrows on rows. Home has none, so a plain mouse wheel could not reach anything past the first screen of a row.
  • Library badge on recents and search results.
  • Your library shows titles that are also in Continue watching (the server leaves them out of library), so the grid has to join the two.
  • Episodes: Specials, Mark season watched, Hide or Show names (spoilers, showEpisodeTitles), season tabs showing a finished season, on-disk label, opening on the first unwatched season. Home showed names always, ignoring the setting.
  • Play failures: no stream (why, and what to do), episode fallback (Start here?), offline (Try again). Home only flashed a line.
  • Nothing found to play yet (playable:false).
  • Grey out trending (greyTrending setting). Home ignored it.
  • Tokens (pips) and hours in the budget line.
  • Instant paint from the last Home seen, skeletons the first time, refresh when the window regains focus. Home showed the mock's Ted Lasso until the server answered.

Already on Home or Detail

  • Recently watched (as Continue watching), TV and Films, library add and remove, Remove from recents, IMDb, Episodes, Mark seen, X-ray.

Stays on the old screen

  • Settings (classic, go settings): limits, media folders, add-ons, keys, grey setting, names setting, player. No design yet.
  • The limit flow: onboarding, tonight's commitment, between episodes, end of plan, wait, unlocked, goodnight. /api/play answering plan-done still hands over to it.
  • Browse everything goes; the classic message stays for those two.

Decisions

Search is a layer, not a screen change

The bar sits in the new empty band above the title, so it can't cover the title, and Home keeps its own DOM underneath. The results layer covers Home only once there's a query. That is what makes Esc restore Home exactly, with no reload.

Rejected: rebuilding Home's rows with results (that was the old way and why Esc reloaded everything).

Search looks in TV and Films at once

A title said into the mic on the TV tab shouldn't come back with nothing because it's a film. The current tab's results come first in each group.

"Library first" means your real library

/api/search calls local the files on disk, which is empty on the VPS. So library matches come from the Home data, instantly, plus any remote hit whose id is in inLibrary. Then On this Mac, then Everything else.

⌘F during playback

Does not open search: the mic would hear the film. The player shows one short line instead, so the press still lands.

Row arrows stay visible

Rule: sideways lists on a scrolling page leave the wheel alone, so mouse users need real controls.
