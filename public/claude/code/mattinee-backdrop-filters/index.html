Mattinee backdrop filters

Mockups

You asked for filter ideas that hide low-res art and calm the area behind text. I rendered Today plus four options (Film, Frost, Ink, Print) on three real TMDB backdrops, Home and Detail, at 1920×1080: https://matt-pages.vercel.app/cd826306-7917-4737-a21f-ba676b98b908/

Decision: Film, because it keeps colour and identity while hiding a 500 px blow-up. Print was rejected as the pick: heaviest and may shimmer if the picture is scaled.

Not tested: cost figures are estimates, nothing ran on the Streamer. Low-res case (500 px) is worse than today's 1280 px minimum. Layouts are approximate. Source: pages site/backdrop-filters, script tmp/bf/r.py. To undo, remove that folder and redeploy.
