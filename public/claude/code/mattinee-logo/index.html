Mattinee logo for the Moss style

Plan

You asked for a logo that fits the Moss style. You then said you really like the current M ticket stub and that the other proposed logos suck.

Decision: keep the stub

The brief asked for 3 to 4 unrelated concepts. Your newer words replace that: every option is the M ticket stub. The concepts I had sketched (mascot, leaf play button, couch, sun) were never built and are dropped.

Because these are takes on one mark rather than rival ideas, one agent draws all of them, so there are no separate agents per direction.

Rules for this job

  • Nothing goes into the Mac, iPad or Android builds until you pick.
  • Sources live in app/logo/moss/, built by build.py.
  • Colours are Moss tokens only.

23:50 Four takes on the stub

You asked for a Moss logo and said to keep the M ticket stub. I drew four takes of it and put them on one page. Nothing is installed in any app.

My pick: Soft stub

It keeps the same M and the same two bites, but with soft corners and slightly uneven edges. That's the shape language of the poster tiles on Home, so it looks like part of the app and is still the logo you like.

The four takes

  • A, Same stub: your logo exactly, in Moss colours. It's the safest, but it keeps the hard corners.
  • B, Soft stub: the same M, cut softer. Light mode is lime with a dark M, dark mode the reverse.
  • C, Caprasimo stub: the M from the app's title font, with the bites cut in. It reads as a letter first and a ticket second. Its wordmark also carries the bites.
  • D, Selected stub: the soft M, tipped over, with a lime layer behind it, copying a selected poster on Home. It's the busiest of the four.

Small sizes

Icons at 32 px and below are separate drawings: a bigger M and deeper bites, or the ticket vanishes. All four still read as an M at 16 px. D loses its lime layer at those sizes, and C is the first to blur.

Decisions

  • One agent drew all four, because they're takes on one mark, so they didn't need separate agents to stay different.
  • The dark icon tile is the app's darkest page colour, and the page shows it on the lighter dark green so the tile stays visible.
  • C cuts its bites with an SVG mask. That's fine for PNG icons, but an Android vector version would need flattening.

What failed

pages deploy was refused: Vercel's free plan allows 100 deployments a day and the account has used them. The page is published as a private Artifact instead, with the images embedded. Running pages deploy after the limit resets will put the same page on the Vercel link.

Not tested

These were only rendered here. None has been seen on a real Dock, iPad or Streamer.

How to undo

The sources are one commit in glass-tv under app/logo/moss/. Removing that folder undoes it, and no app uses it yet.
