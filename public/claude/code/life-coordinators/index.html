17:38 The answers are the game now

You said I'd misread you: the games were breaks unrelated to the content, and you wanted them to be the studying, where the balloon you click is your answer. You were right. I replaced them, so the section above on three minigames no longer applies.

How it plays

Each problem's answers become the targets, and hitting one is how you answer. Three modes take turns:

  • Balloons: the answers float up and you pop the right one.
  • Falling: the answers drop one at a time and you tap the right one as it passes.
  • Sweep: a light walks up and down the list and you tap anywhere to lock it on your answer.

Formulas stay on one line and long answers wrap, so even the hardest problems stay readable.

Keeping it kind

Nothing times out. Every mode loops until you pick, so you can think as long as you need. Speed only earns bonus points. Freeze turns any problem back into a still list, with no speed bonus.

A wrong answer costs only your streak. It shows the explanation, and the problem comes back a few cards later.

One risk: in Sweep, a badly timed tap can lock the wrong answer, and it counts as a real miss. If that happens often, it's the mode to drop.

Tested

I played full bites of all three modes in a hidden browser, with real clicks on moving targets, at two phone sizes in light and dark. That caught two bugs, both fixed. It hasn't been played on your phone yet. Saving your score on the real page is also unchecked until you tap.

17:54 Slower game, and a speed dial

You said the game is too tough and things move too quickly. The moving answers were getting in the way of the Logic, so I slowed everything and gave you the control.

Slower speeds

Everything now moves at less than half the old speed. Balloons rise at 18 pixels a second instead of 46. Falling answers take 9 seconds instead of under 4. The Sweep light stays on each answer for 1.8 seconds instead of 0.8.

Your streak also used to speed things up, so doing well made it harder. I removed that. A streak now only multiplies points.

Reading beat

Each problem now starts as a still list for about 2 to 6 seconds, depending on how much there is to read. You can tap an answer in that list and it counts, with full points. After that the answers start moving, and only time spent moving eats into the speed bonus.

The catch is that a quick answer now skips the game. If you read fast, you may rarely see the targets move. I kept the beat short for that reason.

Speed dial

The turtle and rabbit buttons above the answers set the speed from 1 to 5, starting at 3. Speed 1 is half as fast and also stretches the reading beat. Your setting is remembered on that device, so you can tune it yourself without asking me.

Fairer modes

Falling: two answers are now in the air at once, so a missed answer comes back in about 18 seconds rather than half a minute.

Balloons: they start spread out, so all of them are on screen together.

Sweep: a tap just after the light moves on now counts for the answer it left, which removes most of the unfair misses I warned about. A tap right as the list turns into the game is ignored.
