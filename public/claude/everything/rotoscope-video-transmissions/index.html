Rotoscope video transmissions (no diffusion)

23:30 Research: non-diffusion rotoscope options

Matt's idea: film himself in character as "video transmissions" for Glass TV and Slub marketing videos, then auto-rotoscope into a clean, precise, editor-effect look. Hard rule: no diffusion or generative imagery. Freeze is on, so this is research only; nothing built.

Three pipelines

  • A · Flat vector cut-out (recommended first). Robust Video Matting (RVM) cuts him out (people-specific, has temporal memory so hair edges stay steady; Core ML models exist, light on RAM). Then a 3-frame mask median, bilateral filter + posterize to a palette locked for the whole clip, VTracer per frame (colour vectoriser, unlike potrace which is black/white only), SVG rendered back to PNGs, edited in Resolve. Locked palette and fixed tracer settings are the anti-flicker trick. Would be a small Python script.
  • B · All in an editor. Resolve Studio Magic Mask + Fusion posterize/edges, or After Effects Roto Brush 3 + Cartoon effect. Zero code, quickest to try, but reads more "filter" than vector. Magic Mask needs paid Studio.
  • C · Tracked vector puppet. Draw a character once; Adobe Character Animator (lip sync + face tracking) or the free MediaPipe plugin for TouchDesigner drives it. Zero flicker, cleanest, but no longer looks like him.

Rejected or secondary

  • SAM 2: great for props and click-to-select objects, but hard masks without soft hair edges, and slower. Good as a helper for objects he holds.
  • MediaPipe segmentation: fast and live, edges too wobbly for final output.
  • EbSynth: not diffusion (texture synthesis from painted keyframes); listed for a hand-painted variant, but it's brushy, not vector-clean.
  • Potrace line art (look 3): very clean on the silhouette; interior edge lines flicker, so keep to outline + few lines.

Mac fit

M2 Pro, 16 GB. RVM (MobileNet) and MediaPipe are light; SAM 2 tiny works but is slow. All offline batch jobs, no real-time need.
