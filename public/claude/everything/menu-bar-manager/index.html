Menu bar manager: is Bartender the best? (2026-10-27)

16:55 Recommendation

Matt asked whether Bartender is the best menu bar manager for macOS.

Your Mac today

  • macOS Sequoia 15.7.8, 16 GB RAM.
  • Bartender 5 is installed and running. Ice is also installed (not running).

Switch to Thaw

  • Thaw is a free, open-source fork of Ice. Ice's original developer went quiet, and Thaw took over bug fixes and new-macOS support.
  • It does what Bartender does for Matt: hidden and always-hidden sections, hotkeys, a secondary bar for overflow icons, notch handling.
  • Weakness: younger, and fewer automatic trigger rules (show icon when battery low, on a network, etc.) than Bartender.

Why not stay on Bartender

  • Bartender was quietly sold in 2024 to a new owner (Applause Group); the new version shipped with a screen-recording permission and an updated certificate without telling users, which is why many people stopped trusting it.
  • It works fine, and has the deepest rule system. If Matt relies on conditional rules, it's the one to keep. He doesn't appear to.
  • Paid, closed source, runs with screen-recording access.

Why not Ice or Hidden Bar

  • Ice: works on Sequoia, but unmaintained; users report it breaking on Tahoe (macOS 26). Thaw is its maintained successor.
  • Hidden Bar: simplest free option, but only one hide toggle; no secondary bar. Too basic for a full menu bar.

Untested / not done

  • Nothing installed or changed. Thaw's exact RAM use on this Mac is unmeasured (all three are small native apps; RAM isn't a deciding factor).
  • Switching means: quit Bartender, install Thaw, re-arrange icons once. Undo = quit Thaw, reopen Bartender.
